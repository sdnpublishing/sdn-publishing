The Timetable Cipher

Three messages left in the stationmaster's library, each hidden the way someone who lived by the timetable would hide it. No tricks you can't work out with a pencil.

1. The departure board

Every departure is on the minute. Read the minutes as letters: 01 is A, 26 is Z. A blank line is a space.

2. The shunted letters

The stationmaster moved every letter down the line by the same number of places — the number of the platform he always used.

WKH OHGJHU LV LQ WKH ODPS URRP

3. The keyword

This one uses a keyword — the thing every train must obey before it leaves. Each letter of the keyword shifts one letter of the message, then the keyword repeats. (A Vigenère, if you know the name.)

DWUX UYVMX GHP XWAETS KBUAE
