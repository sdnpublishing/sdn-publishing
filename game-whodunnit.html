The Lost Property Case

Someone left the stationmaster's office unlocked at Ashcombe Halt. Four people were on the platform; each was in one room with one object. Six clues. Work out who had what, where — then name the one holding the key.

  1. The person in the lamp room was not the vicar and did not have the timetable.
  2. The schoolteacher was found with paper, not metal — and not in the waiting room.
  3. The commercial traveller was somewhere with a counter, and it was not the parcels office.
  4. The pressed flower was in the waiting room.
  5. The brass key was not with the schoolteacher or the vicar.
  6. The letters were in the parcels office.
